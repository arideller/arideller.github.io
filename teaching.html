Service

Along with Tatiana Sitnikova and Lena Wang, I co-run the Cambridge Episteme Research Group, which explores contemporary issues in epistemology through a speaker series and reading sessions. Past speakers include Daniela Dover, Rachel Fraser, Michael Hannon, Elise Woodard, and Alex Worsnip.

Along with Joachim Nicolodi, I co-convene the Kinds of Intelligence reading group at the Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence, where we discuss work in the philosophy of mind and on the normative questions raised by artificial intelligence.
